Important Notice and Sole User Responsibility

You are completely and solely responsible for all files, videos, audio, and documents that you upload and share through TeraBox. TeraBox is a neutral cloud platform and does not check, verify, or own user-uploaded files.

If you upload or share any illegal, unauthorized, pirated, or copyrighted files, all legal responsibility, copyright claims, and consequences belong only to you as the uploader. TeraBox will immediately delete illegal files and cooperate fully with police and legal authorities when requested.

TeraBox Webmaster Partner Agreement

Effective Date: September 2026 | Official Program Terms

1. About This Program

The TeraBox Webmaster Program allows verified creators to earn money by sharing files and videos. Rewards are generated through verified video views and new user registrations. By joining this program, you agree to follow all the rules stated on this page.

2. Your Responsibility for Your Files

TeraBox does not monitor or review your uploaded files before you share them. When you share links, you agree that:

  • You own the files or you have legal permission from the owner to share them.
  • Your files do not violate anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy, or personal rights.
  • You take full personal and legal responsibility for every file uploaded from your account.

3. Prohibited and Illegal Content

You must never upload, share, or earn money from the following types of files:

  • Pirated movies, TV shows, songs, books, or paid software without legal permission.
  • Any form of child abuse, adult pornography, or non-consensual images.
  • Violent content, hate speech, terrorism, or dangerous materials.
  • Viruses, malware, hacking tools, or phishing links.
  • Private personal information of other people without their clear permission.

If you share any prohibited content, your account will be permanently banned and all pending balance will be lost.

4. Copyright and DMCA Rules

We respect the rights of all content owners. If a copyright owner submits a valid complaint about your link, we will remove or disable that link immediately. Users who repeatedly upload copyrighted content will have their accounts banned permanently.

5. How You Earn Rewards

You can earn money through two methods at the same time:

  • New User Signups (CPA): Earn a fixed reward whenever a new unique user downloads and registers on TeraBox using your link.
  • Video Play Views (CPM): Earn money based on every 1,000 real unique video views on your shared videos.

6. Fair Views and Anti-Cheat Rules

All traffic is automatically checked by our security system to ensure fair rewards:

  • Views from the same person or device are counted only once every 24 hours.
  • Video play rewards require the viewer to watch the video for at least 30 seconds.
  • Fake traffic, automated bots, clicking your own links repeatedly, or using VPNs to fake views will not be counted.
  • Deceptive titles, fake thumbnails, and forced pop-up redirects are strictly prohibited.

7. Withdrawals and Payment Methods

Earnings are credited to your wallet balance once verified. You can request a withdrawal anytime your balance reaches the minimum threshold of $1.00 USD. We provide two official payout methods:

  • UPI Transfer: Fast payout directly to your verified UPI ID (e.g. name@oksbi, phone@paytm).
  • USDT Crypto (BEP-20): Payout directly to your 42-character Binance Smart Chain (BSC) USDT wallet address starting with 0x.

Payout requests are verified for traffic authenticity and processed within 10 minutes to 4 hours.

8. Account Suspension and Closure

TeraBox has the right to freeze or close any account that breaks these rules, uses fake bot traffic, or uploads prohibited content. In case of account closure due to policy violations, all unpaid earnings will be permanently forfeited.

9. Protecting the Platform (Indemnity)

If any third party or legal authority takes action against TeraBox because of files you uploaded or shared, you agree to take full responsibility and pay for all related legal costs, claims, and damages.

10. Contact and Help

If you have questions about copyright, need to report an infringing link, or have legal queries, please contact our compliance team at dmca@terabox.com or privacy@terabox.com.

TeraBox Global Community Guidelines

Effective Date: September 2026 | Content and Community Ethics Policy

We place utmost importance on intellectual property rights and authentic content creation. Program Rules

Monetization & Traffic Compliance Standards

Partner Monetization and Traffic Compliance Rules

The TeraBox Webmaster Program operates on strict fairness, transparent tracking, and automated anti-fraud validation. All creators, partners, and referrers must comply with the rules outlined below to remain eligible for earnings and wallet payouts.

Official TeraBox Webmaster Program Rules

Effective Date: September 2026 | Creator Compliance and Audit Standards

1. Account Eligibility and Verification

Each individual creator or corporate partner is permitted one active webmaster account. Accounts must be registered with a verifiable email address or mobile phone number. Registering duplicate accounts, fake personas, or automated profile farms to capture unearned bonuses will result in immediate disqualification.

2. Video Play Monetization (CPM) Qualification

Video play views generate CPM earnings based on active rate cards under the following conditions:

  • The viewer must watch the video content continuously for a minimum duration of 30 seconds.
  • Views originating from the same IP address or device fingerprint are counted only once in every 24-hour rolling window.
  • Autoplay scripts, hidden iframe views, background loopers, and muted pop-unders are filtered out automatically.

3. New User Referral (CPA) Attribution Rules

Referral incentives are credited when a new unique user registers on TeraBox via your verified invite link:

  • Attribution requires a genuine first-time installation on a distinct physical hardware device.
  • The referred user must complete standard account registration and perform genuine initial platform activity.
  • Self-referrals by the webmaster, secondary accounts on the creator's own device, and duplicate registrations on previously recorded hardware are blocked by our anti-fraud engine.

4. Anti-Fraud and Prohibited Traffic Methods

TeraBox deploys proprietary machine-learning fraud detection to monitor all traffic streams. The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Commercial VPNs, Tor nodes, residential proxy networks, and datacenter IP traffic.
  • Automated click bots, macro scripts, headless browser runners, or emulation tools.
  • Paid click-exchange forums, micro-incentivized installs, or task completion networks where users are paid to download without genuine intent.
  • Domain masking, fake link forwarders, or unauthorized iframe embedding on third-party websites.

5. Approved Promotion Channels

Creators are encouraged to promote their monetized links across authentic, creator-owned channels including:

  • Personal YouTube video descriptions, community posts, and channel links.
  • Dedicated Telegram public broadcast channels and verified group discussions.
  • Personal blogs, technical tutorials, resource directories, and social media feeds.
  • WhatsApp creator community groups and broadcast channels.

6. Settlement SLA, Thresholds, and Payout Rules

Settlement rules and payment channel standards:

  • The minimum withdrawal threshold is $10.00 USD (or as adjusted in your regional dashboard).
  • Supported payout channels include verified UPI transfer and USDT (Binance Smart Chain BEP-20) addresses.
  • Payout settlements are audited for traffic validity and processed within an SLA window of 10 minutes to 4 hours.

7. Audit Rights, Penalties, and Forfeiture

TeraBox reserves the right to review traffic logs, inspect referral conversion metrics, and withhold payouts if anomalous patterns are detected. In instances of verified fraud or intentional policy circumvention, all accumulated wallet balances and referral credits are permanently forfeited.
